Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 2026-08-24

This policy covers the SheetSet Android app and this SheetSet website. SheetSet is maintained by Majkey25. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not email private PDFs, credentials, or other sensitive documents.

Short version: SheetSet works offline. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking SDK, cloud backend, billing SDK, or app-owned Internet permission. Your score library stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share a file.

Information SheetSet handles locally

SheetSet handles PDFs you import, score titles, labels, bookmarks, setlists, reading positions, annotations, language and reader settings, and backup archives you choose. The app uses this information only to organize, display, annotate, export, back up, restore, and share your scores.

App-private library

Imported PDFs, annotations, the library catalog, and settings are stored in SheetSet's app-private storage. They remain until you delete them in SheetSet, replace the library by restoring a SheetSet backup, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Deleting a PDF removes its private copy and annotations from SheetSet and removes its references from setlists.

Exports and backups

When you export an annotated PDF or save a backup, Android asks you to choose the destination. The resulting file remains there until you delete it through Android Files or the destination provider. Sharing a backup creates one temporary copy in SheetSet's private cache and sends it only to the app or service you choose through Android's share sheet. Android may clear cached files; uninstalling SheetSet removes its private cache.

Restore and ScorePDF import

SheetSet reads a backup only after you select it through Android's document picker. A native SheetSet restore can replace the local private library. A ScorePDF backup import adds compatible PDFs and setlists without deleting the existing SheetSet library. Backup data is processed on-device and is not uploaded by SheetSet.

System pickers, sharing, and external apps

Android's document picker gives SheetSet access only to files you select. If you export, share, or open an external link, the selected storage provider, recipient app, browser, Google Play, GitHub, ScanIt, or Buy Me a Coffee processes that action under its own privacy terms. Review the destination before sharing private material.

The optional Buy Me a Coffee link provides no feature, entitlement, badge, support priority, digital content, or other in-app benefit. SheetSet does not receive payment-card or billing details from Buy Me a Coffee.

Information SheetSet does not request

SheetSet does not request camera, contacts, location, accounts, notifications, advertising ID, broad storage, or app-owned Internet access. It does not collect or share personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or document content with the maintainer.

Support and GitHub

If you email support or create a GitHub issue, the information you choose to submit is used to respond, investigate a report, and maintain SheetSet. GitHub issues may be public. Support correspondence is retained only while reasonably needed for support, security, or record-keeping, then deleted when no longer needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the email address above, subject to legal or security retention needs.

This website

The SheetSet site adds no analytics, JavaScript, forms, advertising, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for service operation and security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. SheetSet does not expose those hosting logs to the maintainer.

Children

SheetSet is intended for musicians aged 18 and over and is not designed for children. It has no account, social, communication, or public-content features.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before SheetSet materially changes its data handling. The date above identifies the current version. Send privacy questions or deletion requests about information sent directly to the maintainer to majkeylab@gmail.com.
